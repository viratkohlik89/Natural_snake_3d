## 1. Game Ka Overview & Pehla Step

### Game Kya Hai?
**NEURAL SNAKE 3D** ek ultra-modern cyberpunk-themed 3D arcade roguelite game hai. Nokia ke purane classic Snake game ko modern 3D Three.js engine, procedural synth audio, 16 dynamic environments, active abilities, aur roguelite mutation draft system ke sath re-invent kiya gaya hai.

### Game Start Kaise Karein?
1. Apne browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari) me `index.html` file open karein.
2. Neon Circular Loader 100% complete hone par **"Press Play [ENTER]"** par click karein ya `ENTER` dabayein.
3. Audio synthesize hona shuru ho jayega aur aap seedhe **Main Menu** par aa jayenge!

## 3. Full Game Controls

Game ko keyboard, mouse/touch, aur gamepad controller sabhi ke sath seamlessly khela ja sakta hai:

### A. Computer / Laptop Keyboard:
* **Snake Steering (Solo / 1-Player Modes)**:
  * `W` ya `↑` (Up Arrow) — Upar move karein
  * `S` ya `↓` (Down Arrow) — Neeche move karein
  * `A` ya `←` (Left Arrow) — Baayein (Left) move karein
  * `D` ya `→` (Right Arrow) — Daayein (Right) move karein
* **Versus (2P) Mode Controls (Local 2 Players on 1 Keyboard)**:
  * 🟩 **Player 1 (Left Snake)**: `W` (Up), `S` (Down), `A` (Left), `D` (Right)
  * 🟨 **Player 2 (Right Snake)**: `↑` (Up), `↓` (Down), `←` (Left), `→` (Right) Arrow Keys
* **Active Ability Activation**:
  * `Q`, `E`, ya `F` — Cyber Codex me jo ability equip ki hai usko instant trigger karein (Phase Shift, EMP Blast, Recall).
* **Augmentation Draft Selection**:
  * `1`, `2`, `3` — Har 5 cores khane par jo 3 mutation cards pop up hote hain unhe pick karne ke liye.
* **Game Management**:
  * `Spacebar` — Game ko **Pause** ya **Resume** karne ke liye.
  * `Esc` — Back aane ya pause menu open karne ke liye.
  * `R` — Turant naya match **Restart** karne ke liye.
  * `M` — Sound ko **Mute / Unmute** karne ke liye.
  * `X` — Main menu par **Cyber Codex** open karne ke liye.
  * `Enter` — Match start karne ya death screen par **Retry** karne ke liye.

### B. Mobile / Tablet Touch Controls:
* **Swipe Mode**: Screen par kahin bhi ungli swipe karke turn lein (sensitivity Settings se adjust hoti hai).
* **D-Pad Mode**: Screen ke corners par visual 4-way arrow buttons aate hain jinhe tap kar sakte hain.
* **HUD Ability Button**: Bottom-left corner me glowing ability button ko tap karke active ability fire karein.

### C. Gamepad / Controller Support:
* **Left Thumbstick / D-Pad**: Snake directional steering.
* **A Button / Cross**: Select / Confirm / Restart.
* **B Button / Circle**: Back / Cancel.
* **Right Trigger (RT) / R1 / RB**: Active Ability trigger karein.
* **Start / Menu Button**: Pause / Resume.

### 💡 Input Safety Features:
* **Turn Queue Buffer**: Agar aap do turns tezi se dabate hain, toh game dono turns queue me save rakhta hai. Koi bhi turn miss nahi hota.
* **Anti-Suicide 180° Lock**: Agar snake Right chal raha hai aur aapne galti se Left daba diya, toh snake apni body me reverse ho kar nahi marega — game us turn ko discard kar deta hai.

## 5. Cyber Codex & Augmentations

Ye Neural Snake ka sabse deep **Roguelite RPG System** hai (`X` key se access hota hai):

### A. Meta-XP Aur Meta-Level System:
* Har run khelne par aapko **Meta-XP** milta hai (score, food, aur ability use karne par).
* Har **100 Meta-XP** par aapka **Meta-Level** badhta hai (`Meta Level 1`, `Level 2`, `Level 3`...).
* Meta-Level badhne par Codex me **naye Active Abilities** aur **naye Tier Mutations** permanently unlock hote hain!

### B. 3 Equippable Active Abilities (Keys: `Q`, `E`, ya `F`):
Run shuru karne se pehle Codex me ja kar inme se ek ability equip kar sakte hain:

| Glyph | Ability Name | Cooldown | Effect / Asar |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| `◇` | **Phase Shift** | 12s | **Ghost Mode for 2 seconds**: Snake transparent ho jata hai aur deewaron (outer walls) tatha apni hi body ke aar-paar safely nikal sakta hai! Trap hone par life-saver hai. |
| `⊘` | **EMP Blast** | 18s | **Hazard Freeze for 3 seconds**: Arena ke saare active hazards (patrol drones, rotating spinners, lasers) 3 second ke liye freeze ho jate hain! |
| `↺` | **Recall** | 25s | **Time Rewind (8 Steps Ago)**: Snake ko instant 8 kadam peeche ke time aur position par teleport kar deta hai! Galat mod lene par maut se bachne ka ultimate weapon. |

### C. In-Run Neural Mutations (Roguelite Draft Picks):
Match ke dauran har **5 cores khane par** game automatically pause hota hai aur screen par **3 random mutation cards** aate hain. Aap keyboard se `1`, `2`, `3` daba kar ya mouse se click karke ek mutation pick karte hain:

#### Tier 1 Mutations (Available from Level 1):
1. ⚡ **Neural Link**: Combo meter ka window duration **+30%** badha deta hai (combo todna mushkil ho jata hai).
2. ◈ **Core Siphon**: 20% chance hota hai ki core khane par paas me turant ek aur bonus food core spawn ho jaye.
3. 🔥 **Plasma Trail**: Snake ke peeche glowing plasma sparks nikalte hain aur har step chalne par **+5 bonus score** milta hai.
4. ⮁ **Ricochet**: Outer wall se takrane par snake marta nahi balki 90° angle par safely bounce kar jata hai (har 10 cores par charges refresh hote hain).

#### Tier 2 Mutations (Available from Level 3):
5. ▶ **Velocity Ramp**: Har 5 cores khane par permanent **+0.3 sps speed** boost milta hai (caps at +3).
6. ◎ **Magnetic Pulse**: Snake ke head me passive magnet field lag jata hai jo 3 cell door tak ke cores ko kheenchta hai (Magnet power-up ke sath stack hota hai).
7. 🛡 **Hardened Scales**: Match me pehla wall collision ya self-collision bilkul maaf ho jata hai (free shield).
8. ⚡⚡ **Chain Lightning**: Core khate hi electric shockwave nikalta hai aur sabse paas ke doosre core ko 2 cells kareeb kheench leta hai.
9. ⚯ **Quantum Decoy**: Har 8 cores khane par ek decoy burst hota hai jo hazards ko confuse karta hai aur instant **+150 bonus points** deta hai!

#### Tier 3 Mutations (Available from Level 5 — Game Changers!):
10. ⚛ **Overcharge**: Pure match ke **saare points DOUBLE (2x)** ho jate hain, badle me snake ki base speed +20% badh jati hai. Extreme risk, massive high score!
11. ✦ **Singularity**: Saare power-ups ki duration **+50% lamba** ho jati hai (Shield aur Ghost bohot der tak chalte hain).
12. 🌀 **Void Walker**: Endless mode ke collapsing burn ring ke andar 1 extra beat grace period milta hai bina mare.
13. ⏳ **Chrono Loop**: Lethal hit lagte hi match khatam hone ki jagah snake automatically 10 steps peeche rewind ho jata hai (match me 1 baar).
14. ⚔ **Apex Predator**: Combo multiplier floor minimum **×2 par lock** ho jata hai aur kabhi bhi 0 ya 1 par reset nahi hota!

## 6. 7 Unique Game Modes

Main Menu me **Game Modes (`G`)** par click karke aap in **7 modes** me se choose kar sakte hain:

| Mode Name | In-Game Tag | Score Multiplier | Key Objective / Gameplay Style |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Classic** | *No timer, no mercy* | $\times 1.00$ | Pure classic 3D snake. Walls aur self-body lethal hain. |
| **2. Time Attack** | *90 seconds* | $\times 1.25$ | 90s countdown; har core par +2s add hote hain. Ghost snake rivalry! |
| **3. Endless** | *The floor closes in* | $\times 1.20$ | Har 40s me arena boundary jal kar shrink hoti hai (collapsing burn ring). |

### Detailed Breakdown of Every Mode:

#### 1. Classic Mode (Score Mul: $\times 1.00$):
* **Ruleset**: Nokia Snake ka pure 3D cyberpunk avatar. Na koi timer, na koi artificial hurry.
* **Danger**: Deewar aur khud ki body lethal hain. Jaise-jaise aapka score aur length badhti hai, arena me dynamic hazards (patrol drones, laser beams) introduce hote hain.
* **Kiske Liye**: High-score chase aur relaxing rhythmic gameplay ke liye best.

#### 2. Time Attack Mode (Score Mul: $\times 1.25$):
* **Ruleset**: Match start hote hi **90 seconds** ka countdown timer chalne lagta hai.
* **Time Boost**: Har core khane par timer me turant **+2 seconds** add ho jate hain!
* **3D Ghost Snake**: Is mode me aapka all-time personal best run ka translucent **Ghost Snake** aapke sath arena me race karta hua dikhta hai! Aap real-time me apne hi pichhle record se race laga sakte hain.

#### 3. Endless Mode (Score Mul: $\times 1.20$):
* **Ruleset**: "The Floor Closes In" — Har 40 seconds ke baad arena ki baahar wali boundary aag ki tarah red-hot jal kar shrink hone lagti hai (`burnRing++`).
* **Hazard Zone**: Burning outer tiles par kadam rakhte hi snake instant fry ho jata hai.
* **Strategy**: Space lagataar chhota hota rehta hai, isliye snake ki length manage karna aur center control maintain karna zaroori hai. (Tier 3 Mutation *Void Walker* isme 1 extra beat ka grace period deta hai!).

#### 4. Survival Mode (Score Mul: $\times 1.30$):
* **Ruleset**: "Waves of Hazards" — Har 25 seconds me arena ke andar ek naya lethal hazard (patrol drone, laser beam, rotating spinner ya ground spike) jud jata hai.
* **Scoring**: Is mode me points khane se zyada **zinda rehne (survival time)** se milte hain — har zinda second par continuous points milte hain! Cores khana optional bonus aur length management ke liye hai.

#### 5. Challenge Mode (Score Mul: $\times 1.15$):
* **Ruleset**: 12 alag-alag handcrafted levels ka campaign mode.
* **Progression**: Har level ka ek specific **Core Quota** hota hai (e.g. Level 1: 5 cores, Level 2: 6 cores...).
* **Arena Shift**: Jaise hi quota complete hota hai, arena sound blast ke sath agle layout me reconfigure ho jata hai! Saare 12 levels jeetne par **"Snake Master"** trophy unlock hoti hai.

#### 6. Cyber Hunt (AI Rival Snake) — (Score Mul: $\times 1.35$ 🔥):
* **Ruleset & Rival Spawn**: Arena me aap akele nahi hain! Aapke opposite side ek **Autonomous Red & Plasma AI Rival Snake** spawn hota hai.
* **Smart AI Behavior**: AI Snake continuous pathfinding algorithm use karta hai:
  * Grid me jo sabse paas food core hota hai, wo uski taraf tezi se badhta hai.
  * Wo outer walls, lethal hazards, aur aapki snake body ko intelligently avoid karta hai.
  * Wo aapke food cores chura sakta hai aur khud ki length badha leta hai!
* **Rival Destroy & +500 Core Bounty**:
  * Agar aap AI Rival ko aisi jagah trap (gher) lein ya cut-off kar dein jahan uske paas aage mudne ka koi raasta na bache:
  * Screen par glowing pink banner pop-up hota hai: **"RIVAL DESTROYED — +500 CORE BOUNTY!"**
  * 65 glowing pink particles ka huge blast hota hai aur aapko instant **+500 points bonus** milta hai!
* **Body Collision**: AI snake ki body solid block ki tarah behave karti hai — agar aap uski body se takraate hain toh lethal crash hoga (jab tak Shield active na ho).

#### 7. Versus (2P) (Local 2 Players) — (Score Mul: $\times 1.20$ 👥):
* **Ruleset**: Ek hi laptop / computer ke single keyboard par 2 dost live face-to-face match khel sakte hain!
* **Player Roles & Spawns**:
  * 🟩 **Player 1 (Left Spawn - Neon Green Snake)**: Controls — **`W`, `A`, `S`, `D`**
  * 🟨 **Player 2 (Right Spawn - Gold Cyber Snake)**: Controls — **Arrow Keys (`↑`, `↓`, `←`, `→`)**
* **Winning / Elimination Conditions**:
  * Dono players ek hi arena me spawn hone wale food cores ko race karke khate hain aur lambe hote hain.
  * 🏆 **Player 1 Win (`p1_win`)**: Agar Player 2 outer wall, hazard ya Player 1 ki body se takra jaye.
  * 🏆 **Player 2 Win (`p2_win`)**: Agar Player 1 Player 2 ki body se takra jaye ya wall me hit ho jaye.
  * ⚖ **Draw (`draw`)**: Agar dono players ek hi step par aapas me ya hazards me collide ho jayein!
* **Party / Couch Multiplayer**: Doston ke sath competitive fun ke liye perfect mode hai!
