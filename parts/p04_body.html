How to play

🎮 Steer & Movement

PC/Laptop: Use the ↑↓←→ arrows or WASD keys on your keyboard.
Mobile: Swipe anywhere on the screen to turn. You can also turn on the on-screen Thumb Pad (D-Pad) in the Settings.
Note: Your turn is instant. You cannot turn directly backward into your own body.

🍎 Eat & Combos

Normal Food (Cyan Cores): Eating these grows your snake and adds to your score.
Special Food (Violet/Pink Cores): These give 3x more points but disappear quickly. Grab them fast!
Combo System: Eating food quickly in succession builds your combo multiplier (×2, ×3, ×4...). This multiplies your points heavily! If you wait too long, the combo breaks.

⚡ Active Abilities

Before a run, equip a special ability in the Cyber Codex (X). During the game, press Q, E, or F on your keyboard (or tap the bottom-left button on mobile) to use it. After use, it goes on a short cooldown before it can be used again.

🧬 Mutations & Augments

For every 5 cores you eat, the Live Augment Dock appears on the left with 3 upgrade cards. Press 1, 2, or 3 (or tap on mobile) to pick one. The game continues without pausing. These powers are permanent passives that stack up until you die.

🔥 Pro Synergies & Best Builds

⚡ Hyper-Score Surge Overcharge + Velocity Ramp + Neural Link
Doubles all score gains, increases movement speed, and extends the combo time limit. The absolute best build for topping the leaderboards!
🛡 Immortal Fortress Hardened Scales + Ricochet + Chrono Loop + Phase Shift
Makes you practically unkillable. Forgives fatal crashes, bounces you off walls, and lets you phase through solid objects like a ghost.
🌀 Core Harvester Magnetic Pulse + Chain Lightning + Core Siphon + EMP Blast
Magnetically pulls all nearby food directly to you and drags distant food closer. Makes collecting food effortless!
⚔ Apex Survivor Apex Predator + Singularity + Void Walker
Locks your combo multiplier so it never drops to zero. Power-ups last much longer. The best setup for hard and endless modes.

💎 Field Power-ups

While playing, you will occasionally find glowing items on the grid. Collect them for temporary special effects:

💀 Hazards

Blocks: Solid grey cubes. Hitting them will end your run.
Drones: Enemies that patrol back and forth. Avoid them.
Laser Gates: These will blink right before they fire. Move away when you see the blink.
Spinners & Spikes: Rotating arms and spikes that pop out of the ground.
Pro Tip: Any hazard glowing Red is currently deadly. Stay away from red objects!
